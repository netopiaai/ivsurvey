Hi! Thank you for taking the time to answer Netopia's image advertisement survey! 😊

In this survey, we will be asking you about some basic demographic information, your general views on advertising, and your impressions of different image ads. Your responses are valuable to us and to our efforts of improving our capabilities.

There are no right or wrong answers, so please answer as honestly as you can.

The information you provide will be anonymized and kept confidential, and only Netopia AI will have access to the original data. You may request access to your data at any time, and ask for it to be corrected, transferred, or deleted. If you have any other questions or concerns, please contact us at survey@netopia.ai.

The survey should take approximately 20 to 30 minutes to complete.
